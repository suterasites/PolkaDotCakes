Meet the maker

My name is Dorothy. Most people call me Dot.

- and yes, that's where the name comes from.

Eight years ago, after much encouragement from family and friends who recognised my artistic talents, I took the leap into the world of cake decorating. I'd always had a flair for the artistic but was unsure of the medium to truly express it - until cake came into my life.

What I love most about cake decorating is the unique blend of creativity and celebration it offers. Each day presents a new canvas, with every design reflecting the individuality of the occasion it celebrates.

The best part? Being part of someone's special memory and receiving a heartfelt thank-you that makes the whole thing feel worth it.
